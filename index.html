01 — Member Help Desk

Help & Support

Frequently Asked Questions

Earnings not updated in ledger?

Completed job invoice payouts are processed instantly and confirmed within 10 minutes once client matches check-in. If delayed, contact coordinator.

Why was my job dispatch cancelled?

Bookings can be cancelled by customers prior to worker arrival without penalization. Cooperative policy ensures travel credit allowance is disbursed to the worker.

Customer dispute or pricing dispute?

Do not negotiate rates on-site. All pricing is locked in cooperative ledger. If dispute occurs, click the dispute support button below.
